# Navigation Rules Rule 16 — Action by Give-way Vessel
# Source: COMDTINST M16672.2D, Navigation Rules: International-Inland (USCG Navigation Center distribution, https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/pdf/navRules/NavRules.pdf)

RULE 16 
Action by Give-way Vessel 
Every vessel which is directed to keep out of the way of another vessel 
shall, so far as possible, take early and substantial action to keep well clear.
