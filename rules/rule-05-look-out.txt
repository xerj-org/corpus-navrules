# Navigation Rules Rule 05 — Look-out
# Source: COMDTINST M16672.2D, Navigation Rules: International-Inland (USCG Navigation Center distribution, https://www.navcen.uscg.gov/pdf/navRules/NavRules.pdf)

RULE 5 
Look-out 
Every vessel shall at all times maintain a proper look-out by sight and 
hearing as well as by all available means appropriate in the prevailing 
circumstances and conditions so as to make a full appraisal of the situation 
and of the risk of collision.
